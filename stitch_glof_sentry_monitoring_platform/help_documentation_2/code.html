UNDERSTANDING RISK INDICES

The GLOF Sentry platform utilizes a multi-variant algorithmic approach to calculate immediate failure probability in glacial lake moraines. These indices are derived from continuous telemetry.

water_drop

HYDROLOGIC PRESSURE INDEX (HPI)

Measures subsurface pore water pressure within the moraine dam structure.

THRESHOLD > 0.85 MPa
upload_file

SEISMIC INSTABILITY FACTOR (SIF)

Aggregates micro-seismic events indicating internal structural shearing.

THRESHOLD > 4.2 ΣE
